## Required evaluation artifacts

For each upstream that reaches production code, add:

- an entry in `third_party/manifest.yaml`;
- an ADR recording the decision and rejected alternatives;
- exact version/commit and checksum;
- license and NOTICE obligations;
- upstream health/security notes;
- conformance and contract tests;
- owner and update cadence;
- fork patch ledger when applicable.
