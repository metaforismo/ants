# PRODUCT.md

## What Ants is

Ants is an open-source platform where a colony of specialized agents plans,
executes, verifies, and coordinates long-running software work. Humans open a
thread, describe an outcome, and observe durable runs that plan, write,
verify, and report with evidence. The product's core promise: **you always
know what is happening, why, at what cost, and who acts next.**

## Who this serves

Software teams of 2–30 people (developers first) who delegate coding work to
agents and must stay in confident control of it. They are professional,
keyboard-fluent, intolerant of ambiguity, and often running several threads
at once.

## Visitor mode

The web surface is **Operate** (with Read moments inside run reports):
the visitor completes tasks — check status, start work, steer, read evidence.
Scanability, truthful state, and familiar affordances outrank expression.
Brand lives in precise details, never in decoration that hides state.

## Success looks like

- Within seconds of opening the app: what is running, what needs my
  attention, what finished.
- One screen where a thread's conversation, its active run, live events, and
  the final report are legible together.
- Every action gives immediate, honest feedback; failures name their recovery.
- Login is boring and safe: one button, real identity provider, no token
  ever touches the browser's JS context.

## Constraints (durable)

- Keyboard-first operation; WCAG 2.2 AA floor; reduced-motion respected.
- Responsive desktop/mobile web; no fake data, no placeholder success.
- Tenant scoping and uniform not-found behavior are structural, invisible
  chrome around them is acceptable, lying about them is not.
- Original design system — not a clone of Capy, Linear, or any category
  default look.
